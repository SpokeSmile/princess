## Image generation prompt

Scene: quiet fictional provincial Russian town in late autumn 1996 at blue hour turning into night, empty rain-wet street leading toward a small municipal archive/post office and an old telephone exchange, overhead cables, one dim warm window, a single sodium street lamp, faint ground fog. Wide 16:9 establishing photograph with deep atmospheric perspective. A worn wooden house on the left, distant Soviet-era building on the right. Center and upper half have open dark blue-green fog and sky so large cream website typography can remain legible. Mood mysterious, restrained, believable, lonely and slightly uncanny; no gore, no ghosts, no people, no pumpkins, no romance, no hearts, no recognizable landmarks, no text, no logos, no watermark. Style high quality cinematic analog 35mm film still with natural grain, subdued teal-black shadows and sparse amber light, realistic period details, not overly glossy or fantastical. Do not reproduce Osinovsk artwork. Image needs visible architecture and depth even with a dark gradient overlay.
